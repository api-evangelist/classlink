ClassLink offers four ClassLink Academy courses to help administrators prevent back-to-school technology problems: "CMC: Continuous & End-of-Year Success," "CMC: New Academic Year Prep," "Roster Server: End of Year Prep," and "Roster Server: New Academic Year Prep." These cover organizing the ClassLink Management Console, managing LaunchPad, checking sync thresholds, and preparing systems for smooth year transitions.
